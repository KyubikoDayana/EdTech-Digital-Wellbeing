MODULE 5: BUILDING A SUSTAINABLE DIGITAL LIFESTYLE

This final module summarizes key ideas and provides tools to maintain healthy digital habits in the long term, ensuring overall wellbeing.

5.1 Digital Anxiety

Digital anxiety refers to the stress or discomfort we experience due to constant connectivity and social media pressure. Recognizing these signs is the first step toward developing a healthier relationship with technology.

A critical factor is FOMO (Fear of Missing Out) and constant notifications, which activate the brain's dopaminergic reward system. This creates anxiety cycles and a compulsive need to check devices to relieve tension, drastically reducing our ability to concentrate (Gupta & Sharma, 2021).

5.2 Disconnection Strategies

Disconnecting periodically is not a luxury, but a biological necessity that allows the brain to rest and regain control of attention. Creating a personal disconnection plan helps maintain a real balance between digital and offline life.

Scientific evidence supports these actions: a short digital detox, combined with mindfulness practices, significantly reduces stress levels and improves sleep quality (Setia et al., 2025).

Additionally, recent meta-analyses confirm that these disconnection strategies improve subjective wellbeing in a measurable and sustainable way over time (Ansari et al., 2024).
