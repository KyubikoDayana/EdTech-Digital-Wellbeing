MODULE 5: BUILDING A SUSTAINABLE DIGITAL LIFESTYLE

This final module summarizes key ideas and encourages students to maintain healthy digital habits in the long term.

5.1 Digital Anxiety

Digital anxiety refers to the stress or discomfort people may experience due to constant connectivity, social media pressure, or excessive digital information.

Notifications, fear of missing out (FOMO), and the pressure to always respond quickly can create mental fatigue and reduce concentration.

Recognizing these signs is the first step toward developing healthier relationships with digital technology.

5.2 Disconnection Strategies

Disconnecting from technology periodically allows the brain to rest and helps people regain control of their attention.

Simple strategies such as creating tech-free moments, limiting notifications, and prioritizing offline activities can improve wellbeing.

Creating a personal disconnection plan can help maintain a healthier balance between digital and offline life.
