MODULE 4: TECHNOLOGY, MENTAL HEALTH AND BALANCE

This module explores the relationship between digital technology and mental health.

4.1 Digital Stress and Information Overload

Digital stress occurs when constant connectivity, notifications and information flow become overwhelming.

Students today often feel pressure to respond quickly to messages and remain constantly connected to online platforms.

Recognizing these challenges is the first step to developing strategies that protect mental wellbeing.

4.2 Achieving Digital Balance

Digital balance means using technology in ways that support life goals without dominating daily activities.

Achieving balance may involve scheduling offline time, practicing mindful technology use and prioritizing meaningful online interactions.

A balanced relationship with technology helps maintain emotional and cognitive wellbeing.

Minimum requirements for a secure password (Microsoft Support)

  • Length: minimum 12 characters; ideally 14 or more.
  • Character combination: include uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers and symbols.
  • Avoid common words: do not use names, birthdays or dictionary words.
  • Do not reuse previous passwords: each account should have a unique password.
  • Easy to remember but hard to guess: long phrases or creative combinations work best.
