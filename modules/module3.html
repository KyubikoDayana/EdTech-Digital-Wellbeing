MODULE 3: HEALTHY DIGITAL PRACTICES

This module presents strategies that help maintain a healthy relationship with technology.

3.1 Managing Screen Time

Managing screen time is essential for maintaining physical and mental health in a digital environment.

Experts recommend balancing online activities with offline experiences such as exercise, reading or social interaction.

Simple actions like limiting app usage, scheduling breaks, and avoiding screens before bedtime can improve concentration and sleep quality.

3.2 Creating Healthy Digital Routines

A healthy digital routine involves organizing technology use in ways that support daily goals and wellbeing.

Students can use productivity tools to plan study sessions, turn off non-essential notifications and define specific times to check social media.

Structured routines reduce digital overload and help improve academic performance.
