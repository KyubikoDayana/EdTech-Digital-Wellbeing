MODULE 3: HEALTHY DIGITAL PRACTICES

This module presents practical strategies to maintain a balanced and healthy relationship with technology in daily life.

3.1 Managing Screen Time

Managing screen time is essential for maintaining physical and mental health in a digital environment. Experts recommend balancing online activities with offline experiences such as exercise, reading, or face-to-face social interaction.

The urgency of managing screen time is reflected in current data: university students spend on average 3 hours per day on social media, which equals a total of 45 full days per year (Statista, 2025). Simple actions such as limiting app usage, scheduling visual breaks, and avoiding screens at least one hour before bedtime can significantly improve concentration and sleep quality.

3.2 Creating Healthy Digital Routines

A healthy digital routine involves organizing technology use in ways that support academic goals and personal wellbeing, instead of becoming a constant source of distraction.

To achieve this, students can use productivity tools based on the Pomodoro Technique, created by Francesco Cirillo in the 1980s. This method uses 25-minute blocks of deep focus followed by 5-minute breaks, which has been shown to improve efficiency and reduce mental fatigue (Biwer et al., 2023).

Implementing structured routines, such as turning off unnecessary notifications and defining specific times to check social media, reduces digital overload and helps maintain high academic performance without sacrificing mental health.
