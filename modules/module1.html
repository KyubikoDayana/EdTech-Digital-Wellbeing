MODULE 1: THE FOUNDATIONS OF WELLBEING

This module introduces the key concepts and importance of digital wellbeing.

1.1 Concept and Definition

Digital wellbeing refers to the state in which digital technology supports rather than interferes with a person's physical, mental and social wellbeing.

According to the Digital Wellbeing Institute, digital wellbeing is the experience of personal wellbeing through the healthy use of technology.

This concept does not mean avoiding technology completely. Instead, it focuses on using digital tools consciously and intentionally.

1.2 Impact of Excessive Screen Use

Research from the American Academy of Pediatrics shows that excessive screen exposure can affect sleep quality due to blue light disrupting melatonin production.

Studies also show that constant notifications and digital multitasking weaken attention control and increase stress levels among students.

Understanding these impacts helps students develop healthier digital habits and maintain a better balance between online and offline activities.
