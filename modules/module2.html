MODULE 2: DIGITAL HABITS AND SELF-REGULATION

This module focuses on recognizing personal digital habits and learning strategies to regulate technology use in daily life.

2.1 Understanding Digital Habits

Digital habits are patterns of behavior related to how frequently and in what way individuals use digital devices and platforms.

Some habits are beneficial, such as using technology to study, collaborate, or access educational resources. However, other habits may become problematic when they lead to excessive use, distraction, or reduced productivity.

Identifying personal digital habits is the first step toward improving digital wellbeing.

2.2 Self-Regulation in Technology Use

Self-regulation refers to the ability to manage one's own behavior, attention, and time when interacting with digital environments.

Developing self-regulation skills helps students control distractions, manage time spent on social media, maintain focus during academic activities and reduce digital stress.

Learning to pause, prioritize tasks, and set boundaries with technology can significantly improve productivity and mental balance.
