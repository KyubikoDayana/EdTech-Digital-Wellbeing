What is EdTech?

Educational Technology, or EdTech, refers to the use of digital tools and interactive learning resources designed to support education and awareness (Digital Wellness Institute, 2022). In this application, EdTech is used to teach students about digital wellbeing and responsible technology use through interactive modules.

Why is Digital Wellbeing Important?

  • Sleep disruption: caused by prolonged exposure to blue light from screens (American Academy of Pediatrics [AAP], 2026).
  • Fragmented attention: due to constant notifications and digital multitasking (Büchi, 2024).
  • Increased digital stress and anxiety: FOMO and constant notifications activate the dopaminergic reward system (Gupta et al., 2021).
  • Cognitive fatigue: caused by long periods of screen use without breaks (Bailenson, 2021).
